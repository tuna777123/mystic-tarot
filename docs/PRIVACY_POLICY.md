# Mystic Tarot Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** July 27, 2026

Mystic Tarot is designed as a private reflection and journaling experience. This policy explains what information the app handles, why it is used, and the choices available to users.

## Information handled by the app

Mystic Tarot may process information that users enter or generate in the app, including:

- profile preferences and language settings;
- tarot readings, reflections, journal entries, moods, tags, and Journey records;
- app preferences, streaks, progress, and feature state;
- purchase and subscription status supplied by Apple App Store or Google Play;
- diagnostic information needed to investigate crashes or technical failures, when diagnostics are enabled in a production release.

Users should not enter highly sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for reflective use of the app.

## Local storage

The current application is local-first. Reading history, reflections, Journeys, and preferences are stored on the user’s device through application storage. Removing the app or clearing its storage may permanently delete this information unless a future backup or sync feature is explicitly enabled by the user.

Mystic Tarot does not claim that local device storage is a substitute for a secure medical, legal, financial, or therapeutic record system.

## Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple or Google. Mystic Tarot does not receive full payment-card details. The app may receive product identifiers, transaction states, receipts, purchase tokens, renewal status, and related entitlement information required to provide paid features and restore purchases.

Paid access must remain locked unless a store transaction is validated through trusted entitlement verification.

## How information is used

Information is used to:

- provide readings, journaling, Journey tracking, and personalized in-app summaries;
- save user preferences and progress;
- restore purchases and determine feature access;
- maintain security, prevent entitlement abuse, and troubleshoot failures;
- improve accessibility, reliability, and product quality.

Mystic Tarot does not sell personal information and does not use private journal content for third-party advertising.

## Artificial intelligence features

When an AI-powered feature is introduced or enabled, the app must clearly disclose whether selected text is sent to an external service before transmission. Private journal or Journey content must not be transmitted silently. Users must be given meaningful control over what is shared.

## Sharing and service providers

Information may be handled by platform and infrastructure providers strictly as needed to operate the service, such as:

- Apple App Store and Google Play for purchases;
- hosting, entitlement verification, crash reporting, or support providers used in a production release;
- authorities when disclosure is legally required.

Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy practices.

## Data retention

Local content remains on the device until the user deletes it, clears application data, or removes the app. Transaction and entitlement records may be retained as required for accounting, fraud prevention, dispute handling, and legal compliance.

When cloud sync or account features are added, their retention and deletion rules must be disclosed before launch.

## User controls

Users can:

- edit or delete supported in-app content;
- use the app without entering optional profile details;
- clear local application data through the in-app deletion control or device settings;
- request help with privacy or deletion through the project support channel;
- manage subscriptions through Apple App Store or Google Play account settings.

## Children

Mystic Tarot is not directed to children under 13. The app should not be marketed to children or knowingly collect children’s personal information. Store target-audience settings must reflect this restriction.

## Wellness and entertainment notice

Mystic Tarot provides reflective and entertainment-oriented content. It does not provide medical, psychological, legal, financial, or emergency advice. Users should seek qualified professional help for decisions or situations requiring professional judgment.

## Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to reduce unauthorized access, alteration, or loss. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

## International use

Users may access the app from different countries. Where applicable, users may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal information.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the app’s data practices change. Material changes should be reflected in the effective date and, when appropriate, communicated in the app or store listing.

## Contact

Privacy and support requests can be submitted through the Mystic Tarot repository issue tracker:

https://github.com/tuna777123/mystic-tarot/issues

Before public store submission, the publisher must verify that this contact method is actively monitored and add any legally required business identity or regional contact details.
