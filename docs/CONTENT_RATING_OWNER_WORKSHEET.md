# Mystic Tarot — Store Content / Age Rating Owner Worksheet

Release: `1.23.0+33`  
Package / bundle ID: `com.tunabozcali.mystictarot`  
Prepared from the current advertising-only product and repository on 2026-08-11.

This worksheet is a **submission aid, not a preassigned store rating**. Apple and IARC calculate ratings from the actual questionnaire answers. Confirm the exact signed candidate and its visible tarot artwork before submitting either questionnaire.

## Product facts already established by source/release contracts

- Native Android/iOS builds contain Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform.
- Public web remains ad-free.
- There is no paid subscription, in-app purchase, paid pack, loot box, cash prize, betting, wager, casino feature, or paid randomized item in the launch business model.
- Questions, readings, Mystic Mirror notes, journal entries, profile details, and progress are primarily private/local-first data.
- Sharing uses an explicit privacy-safe share flow; Mystic does not contain a public social feed, public discovery surface, comments, likes, follower graph, or in-app person-to-person messaging feature.
- Oracle is a product interaction, not communication with another user.
- The project depends on `url_launcher` for controlled external navigation but does not include an embedded WebView dependency in `pubspec.yaml`. Re-confirm the signed candidate does not introduce free-form browsing.
- Store copy explicitly positions Mystic as reflection/entertainment and says it does not provide medical, mental-health, legal, financial, or emergency advice.
