## 1. Positioning

**Core promise**  
Mystic Tarot turns today’s reading into tomorrow’s reality check — then helps the user see the private patterns that keep returning over time.

**Category**  
Lifestyle / reflection / entertainment.

**Business model**  
The product is free. Native Android and iOS releases are advertising-supported and have no paid subscription. The public web edition remains ad-free.

**Primary differentiators**

1. A reading does not end when the cards are revealed: the 24-hour Mystic Mirror asks what actually happened.
2. Pattern memory surfaces recurring cards and emotional shifts instead of treating every reading as disposable.
3. Journal and personal history are local-first.
4. No account is required for the public web edition.
5. All product features are available without a paid subscription.
6. Native advertising is privacy-gated through Google’s consent system; the website itself does not add third-party advertising or tracking scripts.
7. Five complete launch languages: English, Turkish, Spanish, French, and Brazilian Portuguese.

**Do not market Mystic Tarot as:**

- factual prediction;
- guaranteed future knowledge;
- medical, mental-health, legal, financial, or emergency advice;
- a replacement for a professional;
- subscription-based or paywalled;
- an App Store or Google Play release before those signed listings are actually live.

## 2. Best launch angles

### Angle A — “Come back tomorrow”

Most tarot products end at the reading. Mystic Tarot’s strongest hook is what happens next: the user returns after 24 hours and compares the reading with reality.

**Hook:** `Your reading isn’t finished yet.`  
**Support:** `Come back tomorrow. See what actually happened.`

### Angle B — “Your patterns are already speaking”

Mystic connects recurring cards, emotional shifts, previous readings, and weekly evidence into a visible personal pattern.

**Hook:** `One card is a reading. Repetition is a pattern.`  
**Support:** `Mystic remembers what keeps returning — privately.`

### Angle C — “Free without a paywall”

**Hook:** `No subscription. Keep the whole ritual.`

### Angle D — “Tarot without fake certainty”

**Hook:** `Not a prediction. A better question.`

## 7. Advertising-supported product guardrails

The app’s **revenue advertising** and the ads used to **market the app** are different systems.

Current native monetization design:

- Google Mobile Ads only;
- Google User Messaging Platform before eligible ad requests;
- app-open advertising only after at least three completed readings, after 30+ seconds in background, with a two-hour minimum interval;
- interstitial opportunity after every third genuinely new saved reading;
- cadence persists across process restarts;
- no permanent banner covering tarot content;
- no rewarded ad needed to unlock a core feature;
- no paid subscription;
- no fake “remove ads” product until such a product genuinely exists and is store-approved.

The public web edition remains ad-free.

## 8. Creative guardrails

### Allowed

- actual current app UI and verified screenshots;
- app icon and five launch languages;
- Daily Guidance, Mystic Mirror, Living Journal, pattern memory, Arcana Vault, Mystic Path, PIN/biometrics, export/deletion;
- `free`, `reflection`, `ritual`, `pattern`, `journal`, `local-first`, `entertainment`, `advertising-supported`.

### Not allowed

- fabricated testimonials;
- fake user counts, ratings, awards, press logos, downloads or “#1” claims;
- fake countdowns or scarcity;
- a fake ad-free claim for native Android/iOS;
- a subscription or premium-price claim when no paid subscription is offered;
- professional-advice, diagnosis, healing, factual prediction or guarantee claims;
- App Store / Google Play badges before the signed listings are actually live;
- screenshots from a different version presented as current store evidence.

## 9. Launch sequence

### Phase A — public web / awareness

1. Share the public press kit.
2. Use organic posts and creator demos against the live web edition.
3. Route campaigns to localized landing pages.
4. Test every campaign URL on mobile.
5. Keep public claims within the guardrails above.

### Phase B — native ad-supported release

Do not start store-install campaigns until:

1. AdMob Android and iOS apps exist.
2. Production app-open and interstitial ad units exist on both platforms.
3. UMP Privacy & messaging configuration is published.
4. Production builds use real AdMob IDs and `MYSTIC_USE_TEST_ADS=false`.
5. Consent, ad-load failure, foreground and every-third-reading cadence are tested on real Android and iPhone devices.
6. App Privacy / Data Safety declarations match the final signed advertising build.
7. App Store and Google Play listing URLs are actually live.
