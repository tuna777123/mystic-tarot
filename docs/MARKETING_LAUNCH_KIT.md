## 1. Positioning

**Core promise**  
Mystic Tarot turns today’s reading into tomorrow’s reality check — then helps the user see the private patterns that keep returning over time.

**Category**  
Lifestyle / reflection / entertainment.

**Primary differentiators**

1. A reading does not end when the cards are revealed: the 24-hour Mystic Mirror asks what actually happened.
2. Pattern memory surfaces recurring cards and emotional shifts instead of treating every reading as disposable.
3. Journal and personal history are local-first.
4. No account is required for the public web edition.
5. No advertising SDK or cross-app tracking is included in the current product.
6. Five complete launch languages: English, Turkish, Spanish, French, and Brazilian Portuguese.

**Do not market Mystic Tarot as:**

- factual prediction;
- guaranteed future knowledge;
- medical, mental-health, legal, financial, or emergency advice;
- a replacement for a professional;
- an App Store or Google Play release before those signed listings are actually live.

## 2. Best launch angles

### Angle A — “Come back tomorrow”

Most tarot products end at the reading. Mystic Tarot’s strongest hook is what happens next: the user returns after 24 hours and compares the reading with reality.

**Hook:** `Your reading isn’t finished yet.`

**Support:** `Come back tomorrow. See what actually happened.`

### Angle B — “Your patterns are already speaking”

Mystic connects recurring cards, emotional shifts, previous readings, and weekly evidence into a visible personal pattern.

**Hook:** `One card is a reading. Repetition is a pattern.`

**Support:** `Mystic remembers what keeps returning — privately.`

### Angle C — “Private by design”

For users tired of accounts, feeds, and noisy AI products.

**Hook:** `Your journal does not need an audience.`

**Support:** `No account on the web edition. Local-first journal. No advertising SDK.`

### Angle D — “Tarot without fake certainty”

Reflection-first positioning creates trust and separates the product from exaggerated fortune-telling claims.

**Hook:** `Not a prediction. A better question.`

**Support:** `Read the cards, act on the insight, then check reality tomorrow.`

## 7. Creative guardrails

### Allowed

- actual app UI;
- actual generated QA screenshots that match the current version;
- the app icon;
- the five verified launch languages;
- Daily Guidance, Mystic Mirror, local journal, pattern memory, Arcana Vault, Mystic Path, PIN/biometrics, export/deletion;
- “reflection”, “ritual”, “pattern”, “journal”, “private”, “local-first”, “entertainment”.

### Not allowed

- fabricated testimonials;
- fake user counts, ratings, store badges, awards, press logos, download counts, or “#1” claims;
- fake countdowns or scarcity;
- hardcoded subscription prices before store products return those prices;
- claims that the product diagnoses, heals, predicts, guarantees, or replaces professional advice;
- App Store / Google Play badges before the signed listings are actually live;
- screenshots from a different version presented as current store evidence.

## 8. Launch sequence

### Phase A — public web launch

Ready from the repository:

1. Send the public press kit to collaborators.
2. Run organic posts and creator demos against the live web edition.
3. Use the localized landing pages for campaign traffic.
4. Test every campaign link on mobile before publishing.
5. Keep claims within the guardrails above.

### Phase B — native store launch

Do not start store-install campaigns until:

1. App Store and Google Play listings are live.
2. Final signed AAB/IPA candidates pass the production release workflow.
3. RevenueCat products and entitlement are verified in both sandboxes.
4. Purchase, restore, renewal, expiration, refund/revocation, and pending Android purchase paths pass.
5. Real-device QA passes.
6. Store badge links point to the actual approved listing URLs.
