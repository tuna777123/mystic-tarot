## Short description

## Full description

Mystic Tarot is a private, beautifully designed space for tarot-inspired reflection.

Draw cards, explore thoughtful interpretations, save personal reflections, and follow recurring themes over time through Mystic Journeys. The experience is designed to support curiosity and self-awareness without presenting tarot as certainty or professional advice.

### Core features

- Cinematic tarot reading experience
- Daily and guided reflective readings
- Private journal and reading history
- Mystic Journeys for tracking one life theme over time
- Mood, tag, and reflection timeline
- Pattern and progress summaries
- English and Turkish interface support
- Local-first storage with defensive backup recovery
- Restore Purchases support for eligible subscriptions

### Privacy-first design

Your readings, reflections, and Journeys are stored locally on your device in the current release. Mystic Tarot does not sell private journal content or use it for third-party advertising.

### Important notice

Mystic Tarot is intended for reflection and entertainment. It does not provide medical, psychological, legal, financial, or emergency advice and does not guarantee outcomes.

The app contains spiritual and tarot-themed material but no gambling mechanics, real-money betting, explicit sexual content, graphic violence, drug promotion, or user-generated public social content in the current release.

## Review notes for store teams

- Tarot content is framed as reflective and entertainment-oriented.
- The app does not claim guaranteed predictions.
- Subscriptions must be configured using the permanent product identifiers documented in the codebase.
- Paid access must remain fail-closed until a purchase is validated.
- The in-app deletion control removes local app data but does not cancel store subscriptions.
