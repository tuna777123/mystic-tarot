# Mystic Tarot — English Release Notes

Use this text for the English “What’s New” field in App Store Connect and the English release notes in Google Play Console.

## Release notes — 1.23.0

- Mystic Tarot is now free without a paid subscription; the complete product experience stays open.
- Native Android and iOS monetization is prepared for privacy-aware Google Mobile Ads with UMP consent gating and frequency-capped full-screen ads.
- New installs open in English, Turkish, Spanish, French, or Brazilian Portuguese from the device language before onboarding.
- The language selected inside Mystic stays active through card selection, results, profile, settings, and the private app lock.
- Mystic Intelligence continues to build a private seven-day report on the device from saved readings and completed Mystic Mirror check-ins.
- Optional reminder and timezone preparation does not delay the first usable screen; notification permission is requested only when the user chooses it.
- Device Reduce Motion or Remove Animations settings remain respected by the atmospheric background and card-selection motion.
- Journal, Mystic Mirror, encrypted private transfer, and six-digit app-lock protections remain intact.
