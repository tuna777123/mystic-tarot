# Founder Scale Rules

Mystic Tarot does not buy growth to hide weak retention.

1. Prove activation.
2. Prove the 24-hour Mystic Mirror return loop.
3. Prove D7 retention across three consecutive mature cohorts.
4. Prove advertising LTV supports acquisition cost with a safety margin.
5. Only then scale paid acquisition.

If the product misses its gates, reduce spend and improve the product. Do not compensate by increasing ad interruption.

Canonical thresholds live in `docs/GROWTH_KPI_CONTRACT.md`.
