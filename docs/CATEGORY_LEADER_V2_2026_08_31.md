# Mystic Tarot — Category Leader V2

_Last reviewed: 2026-08-31_

## North star

Mystic Tarot does not win by claiming the most spreads, the most divination systems, or supernatural certainty.

It wins by making one closed loop clearer and more useful than any competitor:

> **Read today. Choose one grounded action. Check reality tomorrow. Build private evidence over time.**

The product must feel premium, cinematic, personal, fast, private, and trustworthy while remaining advertising-supported with no subscription, IAP, paywall, or paid feature gate.

## V2 differentiation contract

### 1. Reality Evidence, not prediction accuracy

Mystic Mirror outcomes are descriptive evidence. `shifted`, `partlyShifted`, `unchanged`, and `unclear` are all valid observations.

Requirements:
- Never display a prediction-accuracy score.
- Never label unchanged/unclear as failure.
- Do not infer causality from a card, action, or emotion transition.
- Require at least three completed Mirror check-ins before presenting recurring Reality Evidence patterns.
- Keep private evidence on-device.
- Show evidence coverage separately from outcomes so completion behavior cannot be confused with tarot accuracy.

### 2. First-session comprehension

Within the first 60–90 seconds the user must understand:
1. draw a reading,
2. choose one grounded action,
3. return after 24 hours,
4. record what actually happened,
5. let repeated evidence reveal patterns.

No ad may interrupt onboarding, first card selection, first reveal, first save, or first Mirror explanation.

### 3. Reading quality

Every result must contain:
- explainable card symbolism,
- synthesis across positions rather than isolated card blurbs,
- explicit reflection framing rather than deterministic prediction,
- one visible aligned action,
- a clear 24-hour continuation into Mystic Mirror,
- optional Oracle continuation that preserves context without pretending remote intelligence when none is used.

### 4. Journal and pattern utility

Mystic must meet or beat category leaders on finding and understanding prior readings:
- timeline,
- search,
- recurring-card evidence,
- emotional-state history,
- Reality Evidence outcomes,
- emotion transitions,
- private memory map,
- clear low-data states,
- no fabricated insight when history is insufficient.

### 5. Ritual and interaction polish

Card selection and reveal should feel tactile and intentional without becoming slow. Motion, sound, haptics, and visual hierarchy must survive reduced-motion and accessibility settings.

Future breadth work may add alternative card-picking interactions or spread discovery only if it improves comprehension and delight without burying the core loop.

### 6. Trust and privacy

- No account required for the private journal.
- No private question, note, outcome, or journal content in growth analytics.
- Generic sharing must never leak private content.
- Product copy must distinguish reflection from prediction.
- Data export, transfer, recovery, deletion, and app lock remain first-class product quality surfaces.

### 7. Advertising quality

Advertising supports the free product; it does not define it.

- UMP/consent first.
- No full-screen ad inside ritual/reveal/reflection moments.
- Natural completion boundaries only.
- Cross-format cooldowns remain enforced.
- Public web remains ad-free.
- No dark-pattern retention or ad pressure.

## V2 priority order

### P0 — Strengthen the moat
- Replace prediction-like Insights metrics with transparent Reality Evidence.
- Add minimum-evidence thresholds.
- Make reading → tomorrow → evidence continuity unmissable.
- Improve low-data and honest-no-change states.

### P1 — First-session and navigation polish
- Reduce cognitive load in onboarding/home.
- Make the primary next action obvious at every state.
- Audit hierarchy across Read / Path / Journal / You.

### P2 — Reading and ritual superiority
- Benchmark card selection latency, tactile feedback, reveal pacing, result scannability, and aligned-action clarity on physical devices.
- Improve interpretation synthesis where repetition or generic phrasing remains.

### P3 — Breadth only where it earns retention
- Spread discovery and favorites.
- Better filtering/search dimensions.
- Optional alternative card-picking interaction.
- Custom/freeform spread exploration only after the core loop proves retention.

### P4 — Store conversion and growth
- Refresh screenshots and store copy around the evidence loop.
- Localize benefit-first messaging in EN/TR/ES/FR/PT-BR.
- Measure onboarding → first saved reading, Mirror return, D1/D7, and crash-free sessions before scaling paid acquisition.

## Release-quality gates

Source quality alone is not enough to call the app a category leader.

Required quantitative gates before meaningful paid scaling:
- onboarding → first saved reading: >= 75%
- D1 retention: >= 30%
- D7 retention: >= 15%
- mature Mystic Mirror completion within 72h: >= 35%
- crash-free sessions: >= 99.5%

Required qualitative gate:
- Compare exact signed iOS and Android candidates on physical devices against current category leaders for onboarding, selection, reveal, result, return-to-Mirror, Journal Insights, search, and ad transitions.
- Any moment that feels slower, cheaper, less clear, less personal, or less trustworthy becomes a release-polish issue before feature expansion.
