# Mystic Tarot — Investment Review Decision

Date: **2026-08-09**

## Decision

**CONDITIONAL INVEST — prove retention before scaling capital.**

The product has a differentiated core worth testing: Mystic Mirror converts a tarot reading into a 24-hour reality check and then into private longitudinal pattern evidence. The product is not approved for material paid acquisition merely because source quality and store readiness are strong.

## What this review changed in product

- Due Mystic Mirror now outranks creation of a new Daily reading.
- Living Journal no longer presents a Premium unlock or paid pattern-map CTA.
- Pattern Lab explains that richer insight is earned by evidence, not payment.
- Completed Mirrors have an earned generic share action.
- Default Mirror sharing excludes private reading content by construction.
- A privacy-safe business-metrics boundary rejects private/free-text dimensions.
- Telemetry reporter failure cannot block product use.
- Launch verification rejects known stale paid-model phrases.
- Company KPI and paid-acquisition gates are versioned in the repository.

## Capital decision rule

Do not materially scale paid acquisition until three consecutive mature cohorts meet:

- D7 retention >=15%; and
- eligible Mystic Mirror completion within 72 hours >=35%.

Once advertising-revenue history is mature enough, require projected 90-day ad LTV >=1.5x blended CAC before scaling.

## What is not guaranteed

Revenue, virality, ratings, user retention and investment returns are market outcomes and cannot be guaranteed by source code. What the company can make deterministic is the operating discipline: if retention/economics fail the defined gates, capital does not scale blindly.
