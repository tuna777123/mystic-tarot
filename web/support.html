MYSTIC TAROT

Support

Help with readings, your journal, privacy, installation, purchases, or Mystic Plus access.

Quick fixes

  • Restart the native app or refresh the web page if it remains on the loading screen.
  • Use a current version of iOS, Android, Safari, Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
  • Do not clear app/site data if you want to keep your local journal.
  • Export your journal from Profile → Privacy & data before changing devices.

Mystic Plus purchase help

  • Confirm that the same Apple ID or Google account used for the purchase is active on the device.
  • Open Mystic Plus and choose Restore to refresh an existing subscription.
  • Check the subscription in App Store or Google Play account settings for renewal, cancellation, billing, or refund status.
  • A store or network delay may temporarily postpone an entitlement update. Restart the app and use Restore again after the store status updates.

Mystic Tarot cannot view full payment-card details or directly issue Apple or Google refunds.

Install on your phone

On iPhone, open Mystic Tarot in Safari, choose Share, then “Add to Home Screen.” On supported Android browsers, use the Install prompt or browser menu. The public web release does not process native App Store or Google Play subscriptions.

Report a problem

Open a support request in the public Mystic Tarot issue tracker. Include the app version, device model, operating-system version, store platform, and a description of what happened.

Never include private journal text, receipts, order numbers, purchase tokens, payment information, passwords, or other sensitive data in a public issue. For a billing or privacy request, ask for private contact instructions without posting those details.

Delete data

Open Profile → Privacy & data → Delete all Mystic data to remove the journal, profile, collection, progress, and settings stored on that device.

Local deletion does not remove transaction records held by Apple, Google, or RevenueCat. To request deletion of RevenueCat records, create a support request asking for private contact instructions. A monitored private support channel must be active before paid store launch.

Emergency support

Mystic Tarot is not a crisis or emergency service. If you or someone else may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
