Questions, answered

Clear before mystical.

Mystic Tarot is for personal reflection and entertainment. It does not claim certain prediction or professional advice.

Is Mystic Tarot free?

Daily Guidance and the saved journal remain in the free experience. Native Mystic Plus plans unlock unlimited deep readings, premium spreads, unlimited follow-ups, and the private weekly report.

Do I need an account?

No. Mystic Tarot works without a required account, and personal reflection content is stored locally.

Does it predict the future?

No. It uses traditional tarot symbolism as a reflection framework and is not medical, mental-health, legal, financial, or emergency advice.

Which languages are supported?

English, Turkish, neutral international Spanish, French, and Brazilian Portuguese are supported across the full launch experience.
