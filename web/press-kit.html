What makes it different

  • Daily Guidance with a practical aligned action.
  • 24-hour Mystic Mirror: return tomorrow and compare the reading with reality.
  • Pattern memory that surfaces recurring cards and emotional shifts over time.
  • Private local journal, export, deletion, six-digit PIN, and optional supported-device biometrics.
  • Mystic Path, Inner Constellation, Arcana Vault, rituals, streaks, XP, and achievements.
  • Reflection and entertainment positioning — no factual prediction or professional-advice claim.

Business model & privacy

  • No paid subscription. Formerly gated product features are available without payment.
  • Native apps are advertising-supported through Google Mobile Ads.
  • The public web edition remains ad-free and requires no account.
  • No account is required for the public web experience.
  • Private reading questions, journal text, profile data, and PIN information are not sent to a Mystic Tarot server.
  • Native advertising requests are gated by Google’s User Messaging Platform consent state where applicable.
  • Privacy, Terms, and Support are published for all five launch languages.

Share-ready short copy

Mystic Tarot is a private tarot ritual that asks you to come back after 24 hours and compare the reading with what actually happened. Over time it turns those check-ins into an explainable personal pattern — without requiring an account. Native apps are free and advertising-supported; the public web edition is ad-free.
