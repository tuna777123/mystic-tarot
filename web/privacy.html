MYSTIC TAROT

Privacy Policy

Your inner world should remain yours. Mystic Tarot keeps reflective content local and limits remote processing to what is needed for secure subscriptions.

Information stored on your device

Mystic Tarot stores your chosen name, intention, reading journal, card collection, streak, XP, rituals, and preferences locally on your device. This reflective content is used to personalize the experience and is not sent to Mystic Tarot or RevenueCat.

Subscription information

The native iOS and Android apps use RevenueCat to validate and manage subscriptions. RevenueCat may process an automatically generated anonymous App User ID, purchase history, product and entitlement status, renewal or expiration information, Apple receipts or Google purchase tokens, store locale and currency, and limited device or operating-system information needed to operate the subscription service.

This information is used for app functionality, fraud prevention, customer purchase history, restore access, and subscription analytics. Mystic Tarot does not send your tarot questions, card readings, journal entries, chosen name, or intention to RevenueCat.

Accounts, advertising, and tracking

Mystic Tarot does not require an account and does not provide RevenueCat with your email address or a custom identity. The current app does not include advertising SDKs, does not sell personal information, and does not use subscription data to track you across apps or websites. Automatic advertising/device-identifier collection and RevenueCat diagnostics are disabled in the app configuration.

Payments

Apple or Google processes payment credentials and billing. Mystic Tarot and RevenueCat do not receive your full payment-card details. Store terms govern charging, renewal, cancellation, and refunds. The public web release does not process native App Store or Google Play subscriptions.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them from “Privacy & data,” clear site/app storage, or uninstall the app. The in-app deletion control removes the journal, profile, collection, progress, and settings stored by Mystic Tarot on that device.

Deleting local Mystic data does not delete transaction records held by Apple, Google, or RevenueCat. For a privacy or RevenueCat-record deletion request, use the support page. Never post a receipt, purchase token, payment detail, or other sensitive information in a public issue.

Service providers

Apple, Google, and RevenueCat act as service providers for store billing, receipt validation, entitlement status, and related subscription analytics. Their own terms and privacy notices also apply to information they process.

Children

Mystic Tarot is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Wellbeing and high-stakes decisions

Readings are symbolic prompts for reflection and entertainment. They are not medical, mental-health, legal, financial, or emergency advice.

Changes

Material changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date before they take effect.

Contact

For privacy, billing, or deletion questions, use the support page.
