MYSTIC TAROT

Privacy Policy

Mystic Tarot keeps reflective content local-first. Native Android and iOS releases are free and advertising-supported; the public web edition remains ad-free.

Information stored on your device

Your chosen name, intention, readings, journal, Mystic Mirror reflections, card collection, progress and preferences are stored locally for the product experience. Mystic Tarot does not operate an account server that receives your tarot questions or private journal text.

Native advertising

The native Android and iOS apps use Google Mobile Ads to display advertising and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable advertising privacy choices. Depending on your region, device, consent choices and Google’s services, advertising-related information such as device/app information, IP-derived information, advertising identifiers or consent signals may be processed by Google.

The app requests current consent information before eligible ad requests and checks whether the consent system allows advertising requests. The public web edition does not embed the native Google Mobile Ads SDK.

What is not used as ad content

Mystic Tarot does not intentionally send your tarot questions, reading text, journal entries, Mystic Mirror notes, chosen name, intention or PIN to a Mystic Tarot advertising backend for targeting.

No paid subscription

The current business model has no paid subscription or paid feature unlock. Historical purchase compatibility code may remain in the application source, but the advertising-only runtime does not require a subscription to use the product.

Retention and deletion

Local Mystic records remain until you delete them in the product, clear local app/site storage, or uninstall the app. Local deletion does not claim to erase information independently processed by Google or an app store under their own policies.

Service providers

Google may process information as the native advertising and consent service provider. Apple and Google may also process platform/store information required to distribute the native app. Their own privacy terms apply to data they process.

Children

Mystic Tarot is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a Mystic account system.

Wellbeing and high-stakes decisions

Readings are symbolic prompts for reflection and entertainment. They are not medical, mental-health, legal, financial or emergency advice.

Contact

For privacy or deletion questions, use the support page. Do not post private journal content, identifiers or sensitive information in a public issue.
